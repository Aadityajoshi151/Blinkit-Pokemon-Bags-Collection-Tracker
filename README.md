# Blinkit Pokémon Bags Tracker

A free checklist for the Blinkit Pokemon bags. See all 30 Pokémon printed on Blinkit's delivery bags, tick off the ones you've found, and share your collection with friends.

**Open the tracker: https://aadityajoshi151.github.io/Blinkit-Pokemon-Bags-Collection-Tracker/**

- Shows the full list of 30 Pokémon from the Blinkit x Pokémon bag collab
- Tap a Pokémon to mark its bag as found
- Filters for the ones you have and the ones you're still hunting
- Saves in your browser, with no login or app
- Share link that carries your collection to another phone or to a friend
